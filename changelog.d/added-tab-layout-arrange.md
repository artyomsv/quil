---
headline: Tidy a tab's layout in one click, or Alt+drag panes
---
- **Tidy a tab's layout.** Right-click a tab and choose *Layout…* to even out
  its panes, or to lay them out as columns, rows, a grid, one main pane with
  the rest stacked beside it, or a spiral. Every pane keeps running. The same
  six are in the command palette, and can be bound to keys in `bindings.toml`
  (`tab.layout_even`, `tab.layout_columns`, `tab.layout_rows`,
  `tab.layout_grid`, `tab.layout_main`, `tab.layout_spiral`).
- **Drag a pane with Alt.** Hold Alt and drag a pane onto the side of another
  pane to put it there, onto the middle to swap the two, or onto a tab in the
  tab bar to move it to that tab. Esc cancels.
