---
headline: Move a pane to another tab from its right-click menu
---
- **Move a pane to another tab.** Right-click a pane and choose *Move to tab…*
  to send it, still running, to any other tab on the same machine. The largest
  pane there is split side by side to make room. You stay where you were, and
  a tab left empty by the move is closed.
