---
headline: Move a pane to another tab from its right-click menu
---
- **Move a pane to another tab.** Right-click a pane and choose *Move to tab…*
  to send it, still running, to any other tab on the same machine. The last
  pane there is split, alternating direction, so panes spiral in instead of
  forming thin columns. You stay where you were, and a tab left empty by the
  move is closed.
